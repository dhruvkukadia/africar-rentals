Van hire questions, answered.

How long can I hire a van for?

The minimum hire is 28 days. After that, keep the van for as long as you need it on flexible monthly terms.

Do you do daily or weekend van hire?

No. Africar Rentals specialises in long-term van hire in Perth, starting from 28 days.

What's included in the hire?

Insurance, registration and servicing are included with every van. The vans are regularly serviced and roadworthy.

What size vans can I hire?

1-tonne mid-size vans, which suit courier runs and deliveries, and 2-tonne high-roof vans, which suit trades, removals and bigger loads.

How much does long-term van hire cost in Perth?

It depends on the van and how long you need it. Pick a van, choose your dates and send an enquiry, and we'll come back to you with a quote.

Can I hire a van for courier or delivery work?

Yes. Our vans suit courier and delivery drivers, trades, removalists and small businesses that need a reliable van long term.

Which parts of Perth do you cover?

We service all Perth metro areas.

How do I book a van?

Choose a van above, tap your start date on its calendar and send an enquiry with your name and phone number. We'll call or text you to confirm. Nothing is paid online.
